A play control, beside BACK, at x: ±44, y: 104.

It earns its place for one reason: the confirm gesture needs a target a palm cannot land on. A hold of the open face already means BACK on this page and keeps meaning it, so the night confirm needs a button of its own — and the same one, in the same words, as the MUSIC card.

In daylight it is a second, harmless way to do what the face tap already does. Two ways to start is already this file's idiom: the MUSIC card has both a play button and a face hold.

Both buttons stay 66 px. There are only two of them, and the worst corner sits at r = 157, well inside the 172 px safe radius.
