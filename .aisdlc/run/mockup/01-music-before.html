What is wrong with this screen at 03:00.

The middle button starts music. So does a hold anywhere on the open glass — which is what a palm settling on the panel produces. Neither asks anything, and both play at whatever volume was left behind in the evening.

There is no way to silence the room. Pause is the only stop, and REQUIREMENTS.md records that a direct media_play_pause on the Music Assistant entity does nothing during an AirPlay session.

One detent of the dial moves the volume 1 percentage point, and the acceleration streak resets after a 300 ms gap, so a deliberate turn never leaves the first step.

The volume, the transport word and the arc are all correct and all stay. This card is not being redesigned — it is being finished.
