The one screen that must not lie

At night a turn moves two of the four strips. The Wave and Masha's nightstand are not written at all. If the card still said ALL 4 STRIPS while half the room ignored the dial, the knob would read as broken at 3am — so the sub-line names the lights this turn will move, never the lights the selection nominally covers.

The crescent is the at-a-glance version of the same fact for a half-open eye. It marks the night rule is affecting this turn — not merely that it is night. Pick a single zone and the rule steps aside, so the crescent goes with it.

5% is the floor. A downward turn stops here and stays lit; it never reaches off. So OFF on this card always means something else turned the light off — never that the dial got wound down.

Before the first clock sync the crescent is shown and the sub-line reads BED · TOMER, because an unknown clock is treated as night. That state lasts a few seconds after boot, behaves identically to night, and deliberately gets no screen of its own.
