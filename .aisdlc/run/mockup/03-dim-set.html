SET, and a single zone

The ring and the number turn white while SET is open — the existing convention on every card, kept verbatim. Six seconds of stillness or a tap on the face leaves.

With a single zone selected the sub-line names that zone and nothing else, because that is exactly what the turn moves. No crescent: an explicit selection escapes the night rule, so selecting WAVE at 3am dims the Wave. That is the deliberate act the rule is designed to let through.

The pill is lit when the zone it names is on. A hold anywhere on the face toggles the selected zone off — the dial cannot do it, the hold can, and that separation is what lets the floor be unconditional.

This selection does not survive the screen going dark. sel_zone returns to ALL on sleep, so the knob a cold hand finds is always pointed at the whole room and always under the night rule. See the first finding in design.md.
