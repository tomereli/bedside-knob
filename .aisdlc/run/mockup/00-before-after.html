The lights card, before and after

Left is the card on the knob today, rebuilt from firmware/bedside-knob.yaml — every coordinate, colour and font size is the one in the file. Right is the DIM card that replaces it. Both at 1:1; the glass is 360 px across and sits about 15 cm from a sleeping face.

The six chips do not come back somewhere else. Four of them — the four on/off switches — become the SWITCHES card next door. The five dimmable zones move to the TARGET page behind the pill, where a turn walks them and nothing is applied until a tap.
