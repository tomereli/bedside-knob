Turning is the gesture the hand is already making

Five zones — ALL, WAVE, BED, TOMER, MASHA — and a turn walks them, wrapping. A tap commits and drops straight back to DIM with SET already open, so the very next turn dims the zone just chosen. That is the one good thing about the old chips ("tapping a zone points the dial at it and opens SET in one act") kept intact, minus the six small targets.

Walking applies nothing. Passing WAVE at 3am does not touch the Wave. The only thing that changes a light on this page is leaving it.

Each zone shows its own level as you pass — 35%, or OFF, or ——. That is information the pill on DIM cannot carry and the old chip row never had: the whole room's state, one zone at a time, read by turning.

The five dots say how far round the ring you are without anything to read. Five is not a list and gets no scroll affordance; if a sixth light is ever added it goes in the firmware, not into a menu that grows.

Two ways out, both already learned on the playlist sub-page next door: tap commits, hold leaves it as it was. There is no BACK button, because every entry here is a safe choice and committing one is never the wrong answer — and the space it would take is where the dots are.
