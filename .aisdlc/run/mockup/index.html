Issue #3 — an off card must not show a full ring

Every face on these pages is drawn from the constants in firmware/bedside-knob.yaml: page background, arc r = 159 at stroke 10 sweeping 320° from 110°, and the four text slots at y = −100 / −56 / +8 / +76. The today faces are a redraw of what the file does now, not a photograph — the knob is not attached to this session. The two 44 px icons are hand-drawn stand-ins for the MDI glyphs; position, size and colour are the real ones.

The argument

Left is the AC card with the air conditioner off, today. Right is the same state after. Nothing in the middle of the card changed — both say OFF. The difference is that one of them has a full-length ring in the card's own hue at the card's own radius, and hue and shape reach the eye before a word does.

The rule

A track is only ever drawn under an indicator. The dark ring is not a frame. Its one job is to give the bright ring a scale to be a fraction of — and drawn alone it stops being a track and becomes a claim. So off draws no ring at all, and on draws both, with the indicator floored to a length that cannot be mistaken for absence.
