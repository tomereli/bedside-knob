Issue #1 — the LIGHTS card becomes DIM and SWITCHES

Every screen is 1:1 against the real 360 px glass, drawn with the colours, fonts and offsets already in firmware/bedside-knob.yaml. Open 00-before-after.html first — that one is the argument.

Screens

  • 00 — before and after: today's LIGHTS card beside the new DIM card.
  • 01 — DIM, daytime: the resting state, one group call per turn.
  • 02 — DIM, 22:00–06:00: the screen that must not lie. The sub-line names what a turn will move.
  • 03 — DIM, SET open, single zone: white ring, no crescent, the night rule stepped aside.
  • 04 — DIM, everything off: no indicator arc, which is criterion 14.
  • 05 — DIM, no reading yet: grey ——, not amber OFF.
  • 06 — SWITCHES: four 112 px chips, each one toggling its own switch on the spot.
  • 07 — SWITCHES, all off and one gone: the empty state and the unavailable state.
  • 08 — TARGET: turn walks the five zones, tap commits, nothing is applied by walking.
  • 09 — TARGET, ALL at night: the night rule said again, one screen earlier.

DIM and SWITCHES are adjacent on purpose: they are one subject split in two, and a single detent moves between them. BATH and CLOSET are gone as cards — they are two of the four chips on SWITCHES.

What is not on these screens

  • Colour temperature. Every bedroom light is color_temp only, 2000–6535 K, and REQUIREMENTS.md wants warmth riding the same dial. This issue does not write it, so showing it would put a number on the glass that nothing can change.
  • Home Assistant's own names. They are machine transliterations — khdr_shynh_rshy_kh_shynh_qryh_shml. Every label on both cards is written by hand in the firmware, and that is why.
  • Raw brightness, last-changed, group membership, who turned it on. None of it is something a hand at 3am acts on.
  • Notifications, repairs, updates, weather. Already refused in REQUIREMENTS.md; restated because the sub-line is exactly the kind of slot that attracts them.

Percentages in these mockups are sample values. Home Assistant reads were not granted to this session, so no live level was available — the access note in design.md covers what that leaves unverified.
