04 — the states that are not simply on or off

Unavailable, unknown, and SET. Two of these are the reason the fix is a predicate and not a visibility toggle.

Not knowing looks like off, because it is not on

Why the on-test is a whitelist

state != "off" is true for "", for "unknown", and for every future surprise — and every one of those failures draws a ring, which is the bug. Home Assistant's HVACMode is a closed enum, so a positive test is complete rather than a guess:

cool · heat · dry · fan_only · auto · heat_cool → on, draw the ring.
Everything else, "" and off and unavailable and unknown included → no ring.

This fails closed. If the list is ever wrong, a running AC shows no ring: visible, annoying, and the safe direction. The other direction is the defect this issue exists to remove.

SET is untouched

The FAN card has no unavailable state to draw

Its on/off truth is fan_step > 0, derived from the percentage attribute. When the entity goes away that attribute goes NaN, the guard keeps the last value, and the card goes on showing a ring at the last known step. The AC has a text_sensor on its own state and the fan does not, so the firmware genuinely cannot tell "unavailable" from "still running at 4" — there is no face to draw here because there is no fact to draw. The rule already covers it if a text_sensor on ${fan_ent} is ever added, which is about six lines. Adding an entity binding is more than this issue asked for. Worth its own issue.
