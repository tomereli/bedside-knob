The resting state, 06:00–22:00

Tap the face to open SET, turn to dim. One detent is 20 points: three detents cross from 60% to off-limit, five cross the whole range. The old card moved 1 point per detent and reset its acceleration after a 300 ms pause, so a deliberate turn never left the first step.

The line under the number is the whole point of the redesign. By day it reads ALL 4 STRIPS, and one turn is one group call to light.home_assistant_connect_zbt_2_bedroom_led_strips — a single multicast, not four staggered writes.

The pill at the bottom is the only tappable thing besides the face. It names the target and opens the TARGET page.
