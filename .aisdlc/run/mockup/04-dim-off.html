The empty state, and criterion 14

No indicator arc at all. The dim track stays — it is the shape of the control, not a reading — but there is no full-length ring in the card's own hue, which is the failure the criterion names. An arc that fills the whole circle in amber says "everything, at maximum"; drawing it when everything is dark is the worst thing this card could do.

The sub-line still names what a turn will move, because from all-off at night an upward turn brings the bed strip and Tomer's strip up at 5%, and moves nothing else. A downward turn from here does nothing at all: a light that is off is never switched on by turning down.

The pill still reads ALL and is unlit. There is no state in which this card has nothing to say — the target always has a name, and the sub-line always names a set of lights, even when that set is currently dark.
