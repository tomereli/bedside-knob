04 — Muted. The ring is dead, the number is alive.

media_player.volume_mute was called and is_volume_muted came back true. Three channels carry it, because one is not enough at 25% backlight and AC-5 asks for the state, not for a hint.

MUSIC
38%
MUTED

The glyph changes, the border lights, the arc goes flat.

mdi:volume-high becomes mdi:volume-off — a 44 px shape change, the strongest signal available on this card. The border goes to the hot rose, which is the file's own "this thing is active" convention from paint_lights. And the arc indicator drops to the track colour, so the ring reads as empty.

The volume number stays. The level is still real and the dial still sets it. A dead ring beside a live number is exactly "there is a level, and nothing is coming out of it" — and that is the difference between muted and paused, which must never read the same.

Muted and armed at once: the arc obeys mute, the state line obeys the prompt. Both remain legible on their own buttons. That precedence is the whole reason mute needs three channels instead of one.

Starting playback clears mute. He asked for music; delivering silence under a PLAYING label is two states reading the same, which is a bill this repo has already paid once.
