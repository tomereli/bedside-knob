03 — the FAN card, off and its six steps

The FAN keeps fan_step × 100 / 6 and applies the floor of 8 as a clamp, not a remap. Step 1 already yields 16, so the clamp never fires and no on-state moves. It is written anyway so the rule reads the same on both cards — and as a clamp specifically, because that expression is also what goes to fan.set_percentage. Remapping it would let the ring and the fan drift apart, which is what the comment above that call already exists to prevent.

The detent that turns it off

Step 0 is how the fan turns off by rotation. Spinning down from 1, the ring does not shrink to a hairline — it leaves, while the figure goes 1 to OFF and the sub-line empties. Three things change together on one click of the wrist, which is the loudest confirmation this face can give, and turning the fan off with a spin is the moment that deserves it.
