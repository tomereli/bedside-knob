Bedside knob — design B: the glass is a map

Ten addresses, each in a fixed place on the round glass, permanently. Turn with nothing touched and you have the bed. Put a thumb on a place and turn, and you have that thing, for as long as the thumb is there. Let go and you are back on the bed. There are no modes, nothing to cycle, and nothing to exit.

Four verbs, each meaning the same thing at every address: turn sets a level, tap switches on or off, double tap jumps to a saved level, hold commits that place's one large action in visible stages. A turn wakes and acts on the same detent — there is no free first detent and no wake guard.

One layout to learn: middle is the bed. Top is the wave, on the wall above your head, with the air either side of it. Left and right are the two sides of the bed. Bottom is the bathroom, the way out, with music and the closet either side. The outer edge is everything.
