09 — AC. The far wall of the strip.

Wind the ring clockwise with a finger down until it stops and you are here, without counting and without looking. August in Tel Aviv is why this end of the strip is an end and not a stop in the middle.

ROOM 26 · 85M

Target large. Room temperature and sleep timer small, on one line.

±0.5 °C per detent on climate.bedroom_ac, bounded 16–30, and the arc spans exactly that range so the ring's two walls are 16 and 30. No mode names: heat_cool, dry and fan_only are app decisions, not 3 am decisions. If the AC is off, the face reads AC / OFF and the first detent turns it on in cool at its last target rather than nudging a setpoint nothing is chasing.

Hold 1 s restarts timer.mbr_ac_countdown from input_number.mbr_ac_timer_minutes — 85 minutes, the "let it run a bit longer" gesture. There is no AC-off gesture on this knob. Turning the AC off by accident on a 34 °C night is the worst thing this device could do to him, and the timer already is the off switch.

ROOM 26 · 85M is 13 characters at 28 px; the chord at y = +80 allows about 15. When the timer is not running the line reads ROOM 26 and the space stays empty rather than filling with something else.
