Bedside knob — Design A — every state, 360 × 360, shown 1:1

The knob is always the bed light. A turn changes the value in front of you and that value is the bed's brightness unless the glass, right now, says otherwise. A finger on the glass turns the ring into a strip of nine targets that does not wrap: wind anticlockwise to the wall and you are on BED, wind clockwise to the other wall and you are on AC. A tap means "back to BED" and nothing else, anywhere. A hold means "I'm done with this". When the screen sleeps, everything is BED again.

Night colours are literal: no channel above 0x80, no blue above 0x20, background #000000. On a lit monitor these look darker than they will feel through a dark-adapted eye at 3 am — that dimness is the design, not a rendering artefact. Type is exactly 60 / 28 px; 20 px is used nowhere, and 16 px does not exist.

The whole grammar, on one line each

Turn — changes the value in front of you. Wakes and acts on the same motion.
Touch and turn — walks the nine-stop strip. Anticlockwise to the wall is BED; clockwise to the wall is AC.
Tap — back to BED. Calls no service, from any state, at any time.
Hold — "I'm done with this": goodnight on BED, off on a single lamp, play/pause on MUSIC, restart the sleep timer on AC.
One finger down has exactly three futures — turn to choose, lift to go home, stay still to commit — and which one you are in is on the glass the whole time.
