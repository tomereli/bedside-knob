12 — MUSIC, and the title cannot be drawn

The ugly state, rendered rather than described. Montserrat carries ASCII only, and LVGL does not shape right-to-left text. A Hebrew track title is not a font problem with a workaround — it is a row of empty boxes, or a mangled line, unless something decides not to draw it.

AIRPLAY

Fall back to the source. Never to boxes, never to blanks.

If the title has characters outside the compiled font, the slot shows the source name instead. The user learns nothing false, the line still says something true, and the screen does not develop a dead spot. The coffee knob does the same thing for a bean it cannot render.

This slot is optional by design, which is why the fallback is cheap. It is the only place on the device where content arrives from outside the house's own vocabulary, and the only place a string can be arbitrarily long. Everything else on the glass is a number or one of nine fixed words.

Written at runtime, so it carries width: and long_mode: DOTS — a label without both is one long line running off both edges of the disc, and nothing in the toolchain says so.
