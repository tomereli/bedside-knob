How long does it stay hers?

As long as the glass is lit and saying so. There is no separate target timeout, because a second timer would be a second thing to model. The screen sleeps 20 s after the last input, and sleeping is the reset: the target goes back to BED with it. State is allowed to exist only while it is legible on the glass.

Impatient version: one tap. A tap anywhere, at any time, calls no service and means exactly one thing — back to BED. A half-asleep hand mashing the glass cannot change anything in the house.

Turning here calls script.bed_strip_step with target: masha → light.left_nightstand, ±20 points, the 5% floor and the never-switch-off rule applying exactly as they do to BED, because they live in the same script.
