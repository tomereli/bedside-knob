05 — Finger down on the glass, ring wound two clicks

This is the only way a target is ever chosen, and it exists only while a finger is on the glass. Touch and hold still: after 250 ms the ring becomes the map below, so the map is discovered by touching rather than by remembering. Turn while touching: you walk the strip, one haptic click per stop. Lift: that stop is what the ring now controls.

MASHA
40%

The strip, from the wall clockwise:

BED · ME · MASHA · WAVE · ALL · CLOSET · BATHROOM · MUSIC · AC

It does not wrap. Wind anticlockwise until it stops and you are on BED — always, from anywhere, in the dark, with a double haptic bump to say so. Wind the other way until it stops and you are on AC. Those are the two things wanted at 3 am and they are the two things you can reach without counting; the other seven are counted, and they are the ones used in daylight.

The name is at 60 px because during selection the name is the value the turn is changing. Its current level sits underneath at 28 px so you know what you are about to move before you move it. On release the two swap size, which is the whole transition — see 06.

The longer marks at the bottom are the two ends. The gap between them is the wall.
