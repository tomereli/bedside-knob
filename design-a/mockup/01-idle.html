01 — Idle

20 seconds after the last touch or detent. The backlight has faded to zero over 1000 ms and LVGL is paused. There is no clock, no glyph, no dim ring. The screen sits 150 mm from a sleeping face; anything lit here is light pollution.

Nothing. Black.

This is also the only moment the device holds no state. The target is BED, and that is true because the screen is dark — not because a timer reset it. When the glass is dark, the knob is the bed light. That invariant is the whole design.

A turn wakes the screen and dims, on the same motion — the first detent is a real detent, and the light moves before the backlight has finished coming up. A touch wakes and is live immediately. There is no guard window and no free first click: the device behaves the same whether the glass was lit or dark, which is one less thing to know at 3 am.
