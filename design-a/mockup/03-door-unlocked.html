03 — Wake, and the front door is not locked

The only thing allowed to come before the value. It takes the face on wake, for 2 seconds or until the next gesture, then the face becomes 02. It is a statement, not a control: nothing on this device can operate lock.main_door, which supports OPEN and nothing else.

FRONT DOOR
UNLOCKED

Red, no arc, no gesture attached.

There is no ring because there is nothing to set. It does not have to be dismissed — a turn dims the bed and takes the face with it, a tap returns to BED. Nothing is queued behind it and no gesture is eaten by it. If the hand was already turning when it appeared, it never appears at all.

8 characters at 60 px fits inside the 9-character chord at y = 0. #800000: red preserves dark adaptation and carries no blue. This is the only screen in the set that reports a fact about the house rather than a value under the hand, and it is the only one worth waking someone for.
