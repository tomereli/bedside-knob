No modal, nothing to dismiss. The last known level is still drawn, dimmed to #3a2400, because it is still probably true — it is just no longer being confirmed.

Gestures are not silently absorbed. A detent produces a single long buzz instead of a click — a refusal you can tell from a confirmation with your eyes shut — and the number does not move. This is the one place where the haptic channel carries information the screen cannot: a hand under a duvet learns the house is unreachable without opening an eye.

The dot at the bottom of the disc, #800000, radius 5, is the persistent mark. It appears on every face while the connection is down and nowhere else. It is the only badge this design has.
